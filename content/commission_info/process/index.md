If you contact me through BlueSky DMs, then WIPs will be sent using MEGA cloud service, since BlueSky itself doesn't allow sending images in DMs.

Usually I complete the work within a week. I never had queues earlier, so the description will assuming I already have at most one more commission or none at all.

## Initial steps
<ul>
    <li>You send me a message that you want a commission</li>
    <li>We discuss the idea, composition and details</li>
    <li>I'm starting to work on commission</li>
</ul>

## Work in progress
<ul>
    <li>If there was at least some work done on the image during the day, I will send WIP image to you
        <ul>
            <li>
                This allows to find any mistakes earlier and adjust understanding on both sides
            </li>
        </ul>
    </li>
    <li>If some adjustments are needed we will discuss them
        <ul>
            <li>Make sure to find any disperancies in our understanding as soon as possible. Later it will be harder to fix</li>
            <li>If the adjustemts are too late on the stage and/or too big I might add additional fee to the end price depending on the adjustments complexity</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
</ul>

## Commission is finished
<ul>
    <li>Once the commission is complete, you'll be able to make some minor adjustments
        <ul>
            <li>Allowed minor color adjustmets of the whole image</li>
            <li>Allowe fixing of minor issues</li>
        </ul>
            </li>
            <li>Once you confirm that everything is done I provide you payment link</li>
            <li>After you've done the payment, the platform will automatically give you access to your files
                <ul>
                    <li>Your files will be uploaded to the platform and to my MEGA cloud drive
                <ul>
                    <li>I might keep files on the cloud for a long time, but once I hit the space limit I will delete the oldest ones</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
            <li>The list of files that will be included:
                <ul>
                    <li>Full resolution PNG file</li>
                    <li>Full resolution JPG file</li>
                    <li>40% resolution JPG file for web</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
        </ul>
    </li>
</ul>

## Cancelation
<ul>
    <li>You may cancel the commission at any moment, until the work and payment are done</li>
    <li>If you cancel commission after I started the work, you would be able to resubmit this commission in the future to resume the work on it
        <ul>
            <li>This will be considered as a new commission</li>
            <li>If prices were changed before you make a new submission, the end price will be adjusted based on new prices and the amount of work that was already done</li>
            <li>I may decline to resume the work on the old commission's work for any reason</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
</ul>
